The **Cognitive Bias Detector** is a specialized tool designed to help writers and analysts identify potential logical fallacies and emotional biases in their work. 

Built using **Preact** and powered by the **Google Gemini API**, it provides real-time highlighting and detailed explanations for discovered patterns.

### 🚀 Features
- **Interactive Highlighting**: Color-coded segments for biases (Red) and strengths (Green).
- **Bias Dictionary**: Integrated glossary of over 30 cognitive biases.
- **Deep Analysis**: Toggle between "Fast" and "Deep Analysis" modes using Gemini Pro models.
